About Hearth & Sip

Hearth & Sip is a neighborhood cafe focused on slow, simple pleasures — great tea, freshly baked pastries, and sandwiches made with care. We source seasonal ingredients from local producers and craft each cup and bite to be warm, comforting, and inviting. Our space is designed to feel like a welcoming living room where friends meet, stories start, and the little rituals of the day are celebrated.

Drop by for a relaxed morning, an afternoon pick-me-up, or to bring work and linger over a pot of tea. Free Wi‑Fi and power outlets available.
